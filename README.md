# SWR for Clear Sky Revision

[![Game: S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Clear Sky](https://img.shields.io/badge/Game-S.T.A.L.K.E.R._Clear_Sky-orange?style=flat-square&logo=steam)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/20510/STALKER_Clear_Sky/)

[![Status: Stable](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Stable-brightgreen?style=flat-square)](#)

</div>

A custom adaptation bringing the high-quality weapon animations of **Stalker Weapon Reanimation (SWR)** into the **Clear Sky Revision** overhaul mod.

* Damage, recoil, and other ballistics remain identical to their Clear Sky Revision counterparts.
* Gunshot sounds have been replaced for a punchier impact.
* Uses original S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl textures.

## Known Issues

* **BM16 & P90 Walking Speed:** Walking animations for both weapons appear slower than other guns.
* **P90 Collimator Shader:** The reticle renders outside the gun model because the engine lacks the required shader support.
* **Removed Features:** Gun melee mechanics are unavailable, and some weapons lose attachment support due to missing meshes in SWR.

*Due to my inability to work with Blender and the SDK, these probably won't get fixed, sorry.*
